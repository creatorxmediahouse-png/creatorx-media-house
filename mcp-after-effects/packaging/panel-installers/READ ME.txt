After Effects MCP panel
=======================

This panel lets AI apps such as Claude control After Effects on this computer.

Install
  Mac:      double-click "Install on Mac.command".
            If macOS says it can't be opened, right-click it, choose Open, then Open again.
  Windows:  double-click "Install on Windows.bat".
            If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Then restart After Effects and open Window > Extensions > After Effects MCP.
The panel should show a green dot and "Ready for AI clients".
Close it with the x in its top-right corner. It stays closed until you open it
again from Window > Extensions. AI apps such as Claude Desktop can still reach
After Effects while the panel is closed.

Chat with an AI inside After Effects
  Open Settings in the panel, pick an AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or another
  OpenAI-compatible service), paste its API key, and press Save. Then type what
  you want in the chat box, for example
  "Make a 1080p comp called Intro with a title that fades in."
  API keys come from console.anthropic.com (Claude), platform.openai.com (ChatGPT)
  or aistudio.google.com (Gemini). Usage is billed to the key's account.
  Cmd+Z or Ctrl+Z undoes each change.

Or connect Claude Desktop
  Double-click "after-effects-mcp.mcpb" (a separate download) to add After Effects to
  Claude Desktop, and click Install. Then ask Claude: "List my After Effects compositions."

Remove
  Double-click "Uninstall on Mac.command" or "Uninstall on Windows.bat".
